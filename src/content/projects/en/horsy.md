Developed from scratch using **TypeScript**, **Next.js**, and **Material-UI (MUI)**, this podcast search engine revolutionizes how users discover and interact with podcasts. The design was meticulously crafted from a Figma prototype, ensuring a user-friendly and premium experience.

### Key Features

- **Credible Sources**: Displays direct quotes from each podcast episode to provide context and veracity.
- **AI-Powered Summaries**: Provides concise, intelligent summaries of episodes in response to user prompts, saving time for listeners.
- **Deep Exploration**: Automatically generates related questions to facilitate deeper exploration of topics discussed in the audio.
- **Advanced Filtering**: Includes a robust filter feature to select between various podcasters, categories, and topics.

### Project Impact

Horsy.AI captures the essence of modern discovery, turning hours of audio into searchable, actionable knowledge. By combining a clean interface with powerful backend logic, it delivers a seamless bridge between podcasters and their audience.
